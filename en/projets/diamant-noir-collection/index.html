RoleLogo and photography

Context

Diamant Noir Collection brings together high-end villas and a hotel on the Senegalese coast. The brand needed an identity and imagery that matched its venues.

My role

Multimedia lead at Galsen Digital Agency: logo design, photography and content production for a year.

Visual identity

I designed the collection’s logo: a diamond topped with villas and palm trees, in gold on black. It now signs the brand’s entire website.

Photography

I shot every photo in the online catalogue: villas, suites, terraces and gardens.

What was delivered

  • Designed the collection’s logo.
  • Shot every photo in the catalogue.
  • Video shoots and aerial drone footage.
  • Edited the promotional videos.
  • Marketing and social media communication strategy.
